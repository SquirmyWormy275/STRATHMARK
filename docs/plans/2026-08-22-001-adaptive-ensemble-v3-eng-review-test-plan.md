# Adaptive Ensemble V3 Engineering Review Test Plan

Generated by the STRATHMARK engineering review on 2026-08-22.

Branch: `codex/document-handicap-foundations`

Repository: `SquirmyWormy275/STRATHMARK`

The route names below are functional route families. Phase 9 freezes their exact paths
and schemas before consumer implementation.

## Affected Pages and Routes

- Trusted V3 result commands — authenticate, append valid/non-valid outcomes, recover an
  exact retry, reject changed payloads, and preserve same-round evidence freezing.
- Round freeze/close commands — deliberate closure, partial/empty round behavior,
  correction sequencing, first-issued-field epoch seal, and next-round epoch/weight identity.
- Tournament/field revision ingress — open/close lifecycle, monotonic roster snapshots,
  scratches, substitutions, draw/context changes, and whole-field regeneration.
- Card preparation/status — priority, deduplication, stale invalidation, member/assessor
  availability, deadlines, and restart recovery.
- Field assembly — exact roster/context/epoch/bundle binding, degraded matrix,
  disagreement color, Mark-3 rebasing, optimizer fallback, and two-second budget.
- Receipt lookup — byte-stable immutable recovery before provider/model loading.
- Issue acknowledgment — tournament-manager authority, exact receipt-set binding,
  multi-field expected-version validation, all-or-nothing batch commit, ambiguous timeout
  recovery, conflict behavior, and post-issue immutability.
- Settlement/correction — all sealed forecasts scored, nonfinish semantics, append-only
  reversal, exact issued-receipt/field/entrant binding, later-round effect, and no placing
  rewrite; verified historical import remains a separate non-live path.
- Expected-time override — no default scope, whole-field before/after sheet, zero-history
  starting estimate, supersession, and exclusion from fabricated training evidence.
- Approval projection — ordinary green/amber batch eligibility, separately deliberate
  degraded batching, blocked/red/manual isolation, exact causal flags, pagination, byte
  bounds, and independence from cloud/model calls when opened.
- Operational status/controls — dependency-specific readiness, suspend/re-enable,
  emergency stop, disk reserve, queue age, bundle/model warmth, and redacted support export.
- Model-factory status — candidate audit isolation, one-use lineage-bound audit generations,
  authorized signed manifests, deterministic gates, atomic promotion, tournament pinning,
  monitoring, and future-tournament rollback.

## Key Interactions to Verify

- Complete heat-to-quarterfinal-to-semifinal-to-divisional-final-to-grand-final flow with
  every field independently rehandicapped and its frontmarker rebased to Mark 3.
- One heat every ten minutes and five-minute last-heat-to-final schedule, with the final
  field ready within two minutes and no late model result changing an issued sheet.
- Batch review of green and amber sheets while red sheets remain individually isolated.
- One competitor override regenerates and displays the complete field before acceptance.
- Formula, ML, and council outputs remain blind, independently sealed, and inspectable.
- Every valid completion, including eliminated competitors and both faster/slower
  performances, updates only subsequent-round state and scores every eligible forecast.
- Corrected/voided evidence rebases all dependent capability and credibility state,
  invalidates only unissued work, and produces the same current digest as clean history.
- Every result-driven reaction crosses a source-sequence derivation barrier before the next
  round freezes, including through duplicate delivery and restart.
- Every eligible assessor records a coverage opportunity; a selective abstainer cannot
  improve its learned weight by avoiding difficult cases or relabeling failures.
- Two local LLM families run sequentially, cloud work overlaps them, and all council/outer
  availability combinations produce the declared degradation and approval behavior.
- Restart during result append, provider response, job lease, receipt commit, issue
  acknowledgment, projection rebuild, backup, bundle install, and promotion.
- Restore reconciliation across backup/checkpoint/critical issue journal/tournament-manager
  retry, with zero tolerated loss of an acknowledged sheet.
- Tournament-boundary V2 freeze/drain/signed manifest/atomic V3 contract switch, including
  failure at every boundary and post-switch V2 retries remaining audit-only.

## Edge Cases

- Zero-history entrant, unsupported context, missing species properties, sparse history,
  contradictory fields, duplicate identity, wrong roster, and empty/single-person field.
- Exact ties, ceiling pressure, large time gaps, multimodal forecasts, assessor dissent,
  and optimizer failure.
- Rapid improvement, one extreme slow result, sustained decline, alternating form,
  deliberate-coasting simulations, and repeated manipulation strategies.
- DNF, DQ, DNS, timeout, void, penalty-only outcome, correction before issue, correction
  after issue, and correction after round closure.
- Wrong/missing/revoked service credential, body/principal spoofing, rotation overlap,
  oversized body, unknown field, stale expected version, duplicate command, changed
  idempotent retry, and malformed provider output.
- SQLite lock, full disk, corrupt DB/WAL/projection/event/blob/checkpoint/bundle, missing
  model, Ollama 503/OOM/thermal slowdown, cloud 429/5xx/timeout, and expired credentials.
- Wall-clock rollback, DST boundary, process restart, machine restart, and stale worker
  committing after lease expiry.
- Outbox transient failure, permanent rejection, quarantine, repaired replay, archive
  without two-copy proof, and restore with a missing required blob.
- Correction immediately before/after the first field in a round is issued, proving the
  entire round refreezes before issue and remains on one sealed epoch afterward.
- Last active service-key revocation attempt, same-principal rotation, lost-config bootstrap
  with listener stopped, and append-only credential recovery event.
- Content-valid bundle signed by an unauthorized/revoked key, swapped audit report,
  rollback-parent substitution, cosmetic candidate rename, and adaptive audit reuse.

## Critical Paths

1. Authenticate service → open tournament → verify/pin bundle → freeze round epoch →
   prepare all scheduled heats → assemble and review sheets → acknowledge exact issue.
2. Record all round outcomes → deliberately close round → update prospective cards →
   freeze next-round evidence/weights → assemble final within two minutes.
3. Lose an HTTP response after a committed result or issue acknowledgment → restart →
   exact lookup/retry returns the original immutable result without duplicate effects.
4. Saturate or fail every inference lane → result append, issue acknowledgment, receipt
   lookup, and audited manual/degraded construction remain available and explicit.
5. Corrupt/delete projections → verify event/checkpoint integrity → rebuild → reproduce
   canonical state/receipt digests and acknowledged issued-sheet lookup.
6. Build candidate without audit access → separate evaluator opens sealed role → promote
   exact passing bundle → keep open tournament pinned → trigger monitored regression →
   roll future tournaments back without changing history.
7. Correct a result after derived work exists → append rebase/supersession → crash during
   each mandatory reaction → resume through the barrier → compare to clean-history replay.
8. Issue multiple imminent fields together while one receipt races a correction → validate
   all expected versions in one transaction → commit every acknowledgment or none.

## Required Evaluation Suites

- LLM schema, semantic response, identity/order/time-shift invariance, prompt injection,
  privacy-egress, latency, and provider-error suite for every model/prompt/runtime change.
- Formula spreadsheet golden and metamorphic suite for every formula/parameter change.
- ML nested rolling-origin, calibration, subgroup, missingness, artifact-safety, and
  leakage suite for every model/feature/gate change.
- Full-pipeline historical replay, manipulation simulation, joint-dependence, fairness,
  consequence, disagreement, optimizer, degradation, selective-abstention, and audit-
  lineage isolation suite for every bundle promotion.
- Designated-Windows cold/warm/thermal/power/restart capacity suite before cutover.

Baselines are the current champion bundle and the candidate's predeclared locked gates.
Schema validity alone is never an adequate LLM or numeric-model acceptance result.
